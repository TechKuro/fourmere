# Fourmere Public Positioning — V3

Date: 19 September 2026  
Authority: Founder decision / Project Menice Director correction  
Status: CURRENT POSITIONING AUTHORITY

## Why this exists

The earlier V2 copy direction was too cautious. It correctly avoided fabricated claims, but it exposed internal validation status so prominently that Fourmere read like a project asking permission to become a business.

That is no longer the approved public direction.

Fourmere should present confidently as the business it is. Internal Project Menice gate status remains internal unless a specific legal or commercial reason requires disclosure.

## Public positioning

Fourmere is a UK specialist retailer focused on professional and technical products.

The site should communicate:
- specialist rather than generalist retail;
- considered product selection;
- clear product information and buying guidance;
- professional commercial support;
- a serious approach to manufacturer and distributor relationships.

Working direction, not final marketing copy:

> Fourmere is a UK specialist retailer focused on professional and technical products. We bring together carefully selected products with clear product information, useful buying guidance and responsive commercial support.

Supplier-facing working direction:

> Our retail model is built around direct relationships with manufacturers and established distributors, focused merchandising and a long-term approach to commercial partnerships.

These describe the model and proposition. They must not be extended into claims that specific supplier relationships already exist.

## Tone

Use confident present-tense commercial language.

Prefer:
- Fourmere is…
- We focus on…
- We provide…
- Our approach is…
- Our retail model is…
- We welcome trade enquiries…

Avoid public wording whose main effect is to advertise infancy:
- venture;
- being built;
- currently validating;
- supplier validation;
- current phase;
- internal Gate 3 labels;
- we aim to / we intend to where a truthful present-tense statement is available.

Do not add a public “Current phase — Supplier validation” module.

## Truth boundary

Do not fabricate or imply unsupported:
- customers, orders, turnover or sales history;
- supplier/manufacturer relationships or approvals;
- authorised-dealer / official-partner status;
- named-brand affiliation or supplier logos;
- stock ownership or warehouse capability;
- dispatch, delivery, warranty or returns promises;
- team size, offices, facilities or scale;
- testimonials, reviews, accreditations or market leadership;
- category expertise not supported by evidence.

Confidence is allowed. Fabrication is not.

## Ownership

Growth Strategy owns the current market-facing copy pass.

Store & Merchandising remains accountable for the final site composition and brand presentation.

Supplier & Commercial reviews trade-facing commercial credibility.

Finance & Governance reviews specific claims, required legal disclosures and privacy. Governance does not own tone and should not force publication of internal validation status merely because it is internally true.

Systems/Codex implement the approved copy and visual direction. They do not invent business facts or independently weaken approved messaging.

## Gate boundary

This is still a Gate 3 credibility site, not Gate 5 Store Launch.

Do not add:
- product catalogue;
- live SKUs;
- pricing;
- cart or checkout;
- payments;
- customer accounts;
- paid acquisition;
- supplier logos without permission;
- external supplier outreach or automated communication;
- unsupported operational promises.
