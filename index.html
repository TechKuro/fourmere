About

About Fourmere

Fourmere is a UK-based ecommerce venture being built around specialist professional and technical product categories.

The business is currently in supplier validation. Rather than launch a broad catalogue before the commercial model is proven, we are developing Fourmere deliberately: validating product categories, seeking direct engagement with manufacturers and distributors, and building the operating foundations required for a credible specialist retailer.

Our retail philosophy is simple: present products accurately, help customers understand what they are buying, and build supplier relationships that support a dependable buying experience.

Focused, not generalist

We favour coherent specialist ranges over a catalogue assembled simply for volume.

Evidence before claims

Product, delivery, warranty and commercial statements should be supported by current supplier information rather than assumptions.

Useful merchandising

Good online retail should make considered products easier to compare, understand and buy.

Long-term supplier relationships

We are seeking commercial relationships that work for both the supplier and the retailer, not one-off product access.

Supplier validation

What we would like to understand

For prospective relationships, we are typically interested in discussing:

  • Retailer or trade-account suitability
  • Trade pricing and RRP/MAP requirements
  • Direct-to-customer fulfilment availability
  • Dispatch SLA and lead-time structure
  • Order acknowledgement, tracking and dispatch notification
  • Neutral or blind dispatch paperwork where relevant
  • Carriage and surcharge structure
  • Stock availability and data feeds
  • Returns, warranty and damage processes
  • Service, parts and commissioning responsibility where relevant
  • Payment terms and minimum commitments
  • Geographic or channel restrictions
  • Product data and approved marketing assets

These are the topics Fourmere seeks to understand during supplier validation. They do not imply that any terms have already been agreed.
