Our business

About Fourmere

Fourmere is a UK specialist retailer focused on professional and technical product categories.

We build focused retail propositions around products that benefit from clear presentation, useful buying guidance and a considered customer journey.

Our approach combines disciplined product selection, professional merchandising and direct commercial relationships with manufacturers and distributors. The objective is simple: represent products clearly, help customers make informed buying decisions and build dependable long-term commercial relationships.

Focused, not generalist

We favour coherent specialist ranges over catalogue breadth for its own sake.

Clear, useful merchandising

Product information should help customers understand what they are buying, compare relevant options and make informed decisions.

Responsible claims

Product, delivery, warranty and service information should be accurate and supported by current commercial information.

Long-term relationships

We approach manufacturer and distributor relationships as long-term commercial partnerships, not one-off product access.
