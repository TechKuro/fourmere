About Fourmere

A specialist retail venture being built deliberately.

Fourmere is a UK-based ecommerce venture being built around specialist professional and technical product categories.

The business is currently in supplier validation. Rather than launch a broad catalogue before the commercial model is proven, we are validating product categories, seeking direct engagement with manufacturers and distributors, and building the operating foundations required for a credible specialist retailer.

Focused, not generalist

Coherent specialist ranges over catalogue volume.

Evidence before claims

Product, delivery and commercial statements should be supported by current supplier information.

Useful merchandising

Good retail should make considered products easier to compare, understand and buy.

Long-term supplier relationships

Commercial relationships should work for supplier and retailer, not just unlock a product feed.
