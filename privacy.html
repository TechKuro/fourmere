Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Fourmere, operated by Exilier Limited, handles personal data when you use this website or contact us about a trade or commercial enquiry.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Who we are

Fourmere is operated by Exilier Limited. Exilier Limited is the data controller for personal data processed through fourmere.co.uk and related Fourmere business communications.

Exilier Limited is registered in England and Wales under company number 16809898. Registered office: 33 Copper Beech Road, Nuneaton, England, CV10 9FB.

2. What information we collect

The website does not currently use a contact form, customer account, checkout, analytics platform, advertising pixel or behavioural tracking technology.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, business email address, company, role, product range and enquiry content.

Our web-server infrastructure may process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, date and time, requested page, browser or user-agent details, referrer and request status.

3. How we use information

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, discuss potential commercial relationships, operate and secure the website, maintain appropriate business records and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and we do not currently use website visitor data for advertising profiling.

4. Legal bases

Depending on the circumstances, we may process personal data where necessary for our legitimate interests in operating Fourmere and responding to relevant business enquiries, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract, to comply with a legal obligation, or with consent where consent is required.

5. Cookies and tracking

The current Fourmere website does not intentionally set analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, and it does not use localStorage or sessionStorage.

Our hosting and security infrastructure may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms required to deliver or protect the website. If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising technologies or consent-requiring storage in future, we will update this policy and any required consent controls before enabling them.

6. Service providers

We may use hosting, email, infrastructure and other business service providers to operate Fourmere. Those providers may process personal data only to the extent required to provide their services, subject to appropriate contractual and legal safeguards where required.

7. Retention

We keep enquiry and business-contact information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including relevant commercial discussions, legal obligations and legitimate business records.

Technical web-server logs are retained according to the operational logging and rotation settings of our hosting environment. We review those settings as part of our hosting and security controls.

8. Your rights

UK data-protection law may give you rights including access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, depending on the circumstances.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your personal data is handled.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to privacy@exilier.com.
